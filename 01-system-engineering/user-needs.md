# User & Stakeholder Needs

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the initial user and stakeholder needs for the
Connected Blood Pressure Monitoring System.

The needs are intentionally written without prescribing a specific
technical solution. Technical solutions will be defined later through
system requirements, architecture and design.

---

# 2. User Needs

## 2.1 Patient / End User

| ID | User Need |
|---|---|
| UN-001 | The patient needs to measure their blood pressure safely. |
| UN-002 | The patient needs to perform a blood pressure measurement without unnecessary complexity. |
| UN-003 | The patient needs the blood pressure measurement to be sufficiently accurate and reliable for the intended use. |
| UN-004 | The patient needs the measurement process to be comfortable. |
| UN-005 | The patient needs clear feedback that the measurement has been successfully completed. |
| UN-006 | The patient needs to access their previous blood pressure measurements. |
| UN-007 | The patient needs to understand their measurement results. |
| UN-008 | The patient needs confidence that their measurements are associated with the correct time and measurement session. |
| UN-009 | The patient needs the system to protect the privacy and security of their health information. |
| UN-010 | The patient needs the system to identify when a measurement cannot be considered reliable and communicate this appropriately. |

---

## 2.2 Caregiver / Family

| ID | User Need |
|---|---|
| UN-011 | The caregiver needs to monitor the patient's blood pressure when authorized by the patient. |
| UN-012 | The caregiver needs access to the patient's historical blood pressure measurements when authorized. |
| UN-013 | The caregiver needs to identify meaningful changes and trends in the patient's measurements. |
| UN-014 | The caregiver needs to receive notifications when predefined conditions requiring attention are detected, where applicable. |
| UN-015 | The caregiver needs confidence that the measurement data belongs to the correct patient. |
| UN-016 | The caregiver needs confidence that the patient's measurement data is reliable and has not been unintentionally altered. |
| UN-017 | The caregiver needs an understandable presentation of the patient's blood pressure information. |
| UN-018 | The caregiver needs the patient's health information to remain private and secure. |

---

## 2.3 Healthcare Professional

| ID | User Need |
|---|---|
| UN-019 | The healthcare professional needs access to relevant patient blood pressure measurements when authorized. |
| UN-020 | The healthcare professional needs confidence that the patient's measurement data is reliable and traceable. |
| UN-021 | The healthcare professional needs access to historical blood pressure measurements and trends. |
| UN-022 | The healthcare professional needs the blood pressure information to be presented in a way that supports efficient interpretation. |
| UN-023 | The healthcare professional needs relevant measurement information to support clinical assessment and decision-making. |
| UN-024 | The healthcare professional needs to distinguish individual measurements from longer-term trends. |
| UN-025 | The healthcare professional needs visibility of relevant measurement conditions or factors that could affect interpretation. |
| UN-026 | The healthcare professional needs appropriate access controls to protect patient information. |
| UN-027 | The healthcare professional needs confidence that electronically transmitted patient data is complete and traceable. |

# 4. Key Observations

The Connected Blood Pressure Monitoring System is a
socio-technical system involving users, healthcare professionals,
engineering, software, quality, regulatory, manufacturing, supply
chain, data and business stakeholders.

Stakeholder needs will be progressively refined into:

**Stakeholder Needs → User Needs → System Requirements → Architecture → Design → Verification & Validation**

Technical solutions such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, cloud infrastructure,
specific sensors, AI models or mobile applications will not be selected
at the User Needs level unless they are justified by higher-level needs
and requirements.

# 5. Open Questions

The following questions require further analysis before the system
requirements are finalized:

1. What is the intended use of the product?
2. Is the primary user an adult patient, caregiver or healthcare professional?
3. Is the product intended for home use, clinical use, or both?
4. Is remote monitoring part of the intended use?
5. Is the product intended to support clinical decision-making?
6. What markets/countries are initially targeted?
7. What level of measurement performance is required?
8. What patient population is within scope?
9. What conditions could make a measurement unreliable?
10. What information should be displayed directly on the device?
11. What information should be available through an application?
12. What data should be stored locally versus remotely?
13. What AI capabilities are appropriate for the intended use?
14. What AI outputs should be informational versus clinically actionable?
15. What privacy and cybersecurity requirements apply?
16. What regulatory pathway would apply to the intended product?
